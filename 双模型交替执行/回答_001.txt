Let's break down the problem again with a clear understanding of the relationships between the number of apples each person has:

1. **Initial Condition:**
   - Xiao Ming starts with 3 apples.

2. **Apples for Xiao Hong:**
   - Xiao Hong has 2 more apples than Xiao Ming.
   - Therefore, Xiao Hong has \( 3 + 2 = 5 \) apples.

3. **Apples for Xiao Hua:**
   - Xiao Hua has half as many apples as Xiao Hong.
   - Therefore, Xiao Hua has \( \frac{5}{2} = 2.5 \) apples.

Since you can't give half an apple, let's assume Xiao Hua actually has 2 apples (since he needs whole apples).

4. **Apples Given to Xiao Ming:**
   - Xiao Hua gives 1 apple to Xiao Ming.
   - Xiao Ming’s new total = 3 (initial) + 1 (given) = 4 apples.

Thus, after receiving 1 apple from Xiao Hua, Xiao Ming ends up with 4 apples.

So, the final answer is that Xiao Ming has 4 apples.
