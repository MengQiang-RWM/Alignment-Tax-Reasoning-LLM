4. Xiao Hong had 2 more apples than Xiao Ming, so \( H = M + 2 \).

First, let's express \( W \) in terms of \( M \):
\[ W = \frac{M + 2}{2} \]

\[ M - 1 \]

\[ M - 1 = \frac{M + 2}{2} \]

To solve for \( M \), we first eliminate the fraction by multiplying both sides of the equation by 2:
\[ 2(M - 1) = M + 2 \]
\[ 2M - 2 = M + 2 \]
Subtract \( M \) from both sides:
\[ M - 2 = 2 \]
Add 2 to both sides:
\[ M = 4 \]

So, Xiao Ming initially had 4 apples. Now, we can find out how many apples Xiao Ming has after receiving an apple from Xiao Hua:
\[ M - 1 = 4 - 1 = 3 \]
