Let's start by determining how many apples each person has initially.

1. **Initial number of apples:**
   - Xiao Ming starts with 3 apples.

   - Xiao Hua gives 1 apple to Xiao Ming.
   - Therefore, the total number of apples Xiao Ming has after receiving from Xiao Hua is:
     \[
     3 + 1 = 4
     \]

3. **Number of apples Xiao Hua has:**
   - Xiao Hua originally had 0 apples (since he didn't give any).
   - After receiving 1 apple from Xiao Ming, he has:
     \[
     0 + 1 = 1
     \]
   - So, Xiao Hua has 1 apple.

4. **Apples carried by Xiao Hong:**
   - Xiao Hong carries a backpack with 2 more apples than Xiao Ming.
   - Since Xiao Ming started with 3 apples, carrying 2 more apples means she has:
     \[
     3 + 2 = 5 \text{ apples}
     \]

5. **Total number of apples Xiao Hong has:**
   - Xiao Hong also has 1 apple (from her own backpack) plus the 2 apples she received from Xiao Hua.
   - Therefore, the total number of apples Xiao Hong has is:
     \[
     1 + 2 = 3
     \]

6. **Final number of apples Xiao Ming has:**
   - After receiving apples from Xiao Hua and carrying apples from Xiao Hong, Xiao Ming ends up with:
     \[
     4 + 3 = 7
     \]

Thus, the final number of apples Xiao Ming has is \(\boxed{7}\).
