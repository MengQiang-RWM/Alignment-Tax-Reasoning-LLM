To determine how many apples Xiao Ming has after receiving one from Xiao Hua, we can follow these steps:

1. **Initial number of apples:**
   - Xiao Ming starts with 3 apples.
   - Xiao Hong has 2 more apples than Xiao Ming.
     \[
     \text{Number of apples Xiao Hong has} = 3 + 2 = 5
     \]
   - Xiao Hua has half as many apples as Xiao Hong.
     \[
     \text{Number of apples Xiao Hua has} = \frac{5}{2} = 2.5
     \]

2. **Apples given to Xiao Ming:**
   - Xiao Hua gives 1 apple to Xiao Ming.
     \[
     \text{Number of apples Xiao Ming has after receiving the apple} = 3 - 1 = 2
     \]

Therefore, after receiving one apple from Xiao Hua, Xiao Ming has \(\boxed{2}\) apples.
