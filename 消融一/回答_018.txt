To determine how many apples Xiao Ming has after receiving an apple from Xiao Hua, we will follow these steps:

   - Xiao Hong has 2 more apples than Xiao Ming.
   - Xiao Ming has 3 apples.

   - Xiao Hua has half as many apples as Xiao Hong.
   - Xiao Hong has 5 apples.
   - Therefore, Xiao Hua has \( \frac{5}{2} = 2.5 \) apples.

   - Xiao Ming has 3 apples.
   - Xiao Hua has 2.5 apples.
   - Together they have \( 3 + 2.5 = 5.5 \) apples.

   - Xiao Ming receives 1 apple from Xiao Hua.
   - After giving away one apple, Xiao Ming has \( 3 - 1 = 2 \) apples left.
