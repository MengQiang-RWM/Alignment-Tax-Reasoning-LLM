3. **Calculate the total number of apples Xiao Hua has after giving 1 apple to Xiao Ming:**
   - Before giving any, Xiao Hua had 2 apples.
   - After giving 1 apple to Xiao Ming, Xiao Hua will have \(2 - 1 = 1\) apple.

   - Initially, Xiao Ming had 3 apples.
