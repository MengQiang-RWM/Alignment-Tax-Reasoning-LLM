Let's start by determining the number of apples each person has initially.

1. **Xiao Ming**:
   - Xiao Ming has 3 apples.
   
2. **Xiao Hong**:
   - Xiao Hong has 2 more apples than Xiao Ming.
   - Therefore, Xiao Hong has \( 3 + 2 = 5 \) apples.
   
3. **Xiao Hua**:
   - Xiao Hua has half as many apples as Xiao Hong.
   - Therefore, Xiao Hua has \( \frac{5}{2} = 2.5 \) apples (or 2 whole apples and a fraction).
   - Since we typically don't deal with fractions in this context, let's assume Xiao Hua has 2 whole apples and a fraction of an apple).

4. **Apples given by Xiao Hua**:
   - Xiao Hua gives 1 apple to Xiao Ming.
   - So, Xiao Ming receives 1 apple from Xiao Hua.
   - Now, Xiao Ming has \( 3 + 1 = 4 \) apples.
   
Therefore, after receiving 1 apple from Xiao Hua, Xiao Ming has 4 apples.
