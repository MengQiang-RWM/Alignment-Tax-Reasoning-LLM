Let's start by determining how many apples each person initially has:

1. Xiao Ming has 3 apples.

3. Xiao Hua has half as many apples as Xiao Hong, so Xiao Hua has \(\frac{5}{2} = 2.5\) apples.

Next, we need to account for the fact that Xiao Hua gives 1 apple to Xiao Ming. This means Xiao Ming will receive an additional 1 apple from Xiao Hua. Therefore, Xiao Ming will have:

\[3 + 1 = 4\]

So, after giving 1 apple to Xiao Ming, Xiao Ming will have 4 apples. The final answer is:

\[
