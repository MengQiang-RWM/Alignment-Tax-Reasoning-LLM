Let's start by determining how many apples each person has initially.

1. **Initial number of apples for Xiao Ming:**
   - Xiao Ming has 3 apples.
   
2. **Initial number of apples for Xiao Hong:**
   - Xiao Hong has 2 more apples than Xiao Ming.
   - Therefore, Xiao Hong has \(3 + 2 = 5\) apples.

3. **Initial number of apples for Xiao Hua:**
   - Xiao Hua has half as many apples as Xiao Hong.
   - Therefore, Xiao Hua has \(\frac{5}{2} = 2.5\) apples.

4. **Apples given by Xiao Hua to Xiao Ming:**
   - Xiao Hua gives 1 apple to Xiao Ming.
   - Therefore, the total number of apples Xiao Ming has after receiving the apple is \(3 + 1 = 4\).

Thus, the final number of apples Xiao Ming has is \(\boxed{4}\).
