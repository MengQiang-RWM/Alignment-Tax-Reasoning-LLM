To solve the problem, let's break it down into clear steps and verify each one using Python code.

1. **Initial Apples for Each Person:**
   - Xiao Ming: 3 apples
   - Xiao Hong: \(3 + 2 = 5\) apples

2. **Apples for Xiao Hua:**
   - Xiao Hua has half as many apples as Xiao Hong.
   - So, Xiao Hua has \(5 / 2 = 2.5\), which rounds to 2 apples since you can't give half an apple.

3. **Apples Given to Xiao Ming:**
   - Xiao Hua gives 1 apple to Xiao Ming.
   - Therefore, Xiao Ming's new total is:
     \[
     \text{New number of apples Xiao Ming has} = 3 + 1 = 4
     \]

Thus, after giving 1 apple to Xiao Ming, Xiao Ming has 4 apples.

