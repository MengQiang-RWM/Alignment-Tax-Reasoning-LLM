Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. **Determine how many apples Xiao Hong has:**
   - Xiao Ming has 3 apples.
   - Xiao Hong has 2 more apples than Xiao Ming.
   \[
   \text{Number of apples Xiao Hong has} = 3 + 2 = 5
   \]

2. **Determine how many apples Xiao Hua has:**
   - Xiao Hua has half as many apples as Xiao Hong.
   \[
   \text{Number of apples Xiao Hua has} = \frac{5}{2} = 2.5
   \]
   
   Since we cannot have half an apple in this context, let's assume that Xiao Hua can only give whole apples. If Xiao Hua were allowed to give half an apple, he would need to round up or down based on the context, but for simplicity, let's consider he gave exactly half.

3. **Calculate how many apples Xiao Ming has after receiving one from Xiao Hua:**
   - Initially, Xiao Ming had 3 apples.
   - Xiao Hua gives 0.5 (or 1 if rounded) apple to Xiao Ming.
   \[
   \text{Total number of apples Xiao Ming has now} = 3 + 0.5 = 3.5
   \]

Therefore, after giving away the apple, Xiao Ming ends up with 3.5 apples.
