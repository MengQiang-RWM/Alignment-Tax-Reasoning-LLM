Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. **Determine how many apples Xiao Hong has:**
   - Xiao Ming has 3 apples.
   - Xiao Hong has 2 more apples than Xiao Ming.
   - Therefore, Xiao Hong has \(3 + 2 = 5\) apples.

2. **Determine how many apples Xiao Hua has:**
   - Xiao Hua has half as many apples as Xiao Hong.
   - Since Xiao Hong has 5 apples, Xiao Hua has \(\frac{5}{2} = 2.5\) apples. However, since we can't have half an apple in this context (assuming whole apples), let's assume Xiao Hua has 2 or 3 apples based on typical interpretations:
     - If Xiao Hua has 2 apples, it would be less than half of what Xiao Hong has.
     - If Xiao Hua has 3 apples, it is exactly half of what Xiao Hong has.
   
   Let's proceed with the assumption that Xiao Hua has 2 apples for simplicity, although typically Xiao Hua would have 3 if we're considering equal division.

3. **Calculate after Xiao Hua gives 1 apple to Xiao Ming:**
   - Initially, Xiao Ming has 3 apples.
   - Xiao Hua gives 1 apple to Xiao Ming.
   - So, Xiao Ming receives 1 apple and his total becomes \(3 + 1 = 4\) apples.

Therefore, after Xiao Hua gives 1 apple to Xiao Ming, Xiao Ming has 4 apples.
