Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. **Initial Apples:**
   - Xiao Ming starts with 3 apples.

2. **Apples Xiao Hong Has:**
   - Xiao Hong has 2 more apples than Xiao Ming.
   - Therefore, Xiao Hong has \(3 + 2 = 5\) apples.

3. **Apples Xiao Hua Has:**
   - Xiao Hua has half as many apples as Xiao Hong.
   - Therefore, Xiao Hua has \(\frac{5}{2} = 2.5\) apples. Since we can't have half an apple in this context, let's assume it should be rounded or interpreted differently (possibly considering whole apples).

4. **Apples After Giving Away One Apple:**
   - Xiao Hua gives 1 apple to Xiao Ming.
   - Before giving away the apple:
     - Xiao Ming had 3 apples from Xiao Hong.
     - Xiao Hua had 2.5 apples.
   - After giving away 1 apple:
     - Xiao Ming receives 1 apple from Xiao Hua.
     - Xiao Ming now has \(3 + 1 = 4\) apples.
     - Xiao Hua loses 1 apple and is left with 1 apple.

So, after all these steps, Xiao Ming ends up with 4 apples.
