Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. **Determine how many apples Xiao Hong has:**
   - Xiao Ming has 3 apples.
   - Xiao Hong has 2 more apples than Xiao Ming.
   \[
   \text{Apples Xiao Hong has} = 3 + 2 = 5
   \]

2. **Determine how many apples Xiao Hua has:**
   - Xiao Hua has half as many apples as Xiao Hong.
   \[
   \text{Apples Xiao Hua has} = \frac{5}{2} = 2.5
   \]
   Since we can't have half an apple in this context, let's assume Xiao Hua has either 2 or 3 apples. Given that he gave away 1 apple later, it makes sense for him to have 2 apples initially (since giving away 1 would leave him with 1).

3. **Calculate the number of apples Xiao Hua has after giving one to Xiao Ming:**
   - Initially, Xiao Hua had 2 apples and gave 1 to Xiao Ming.
   \[
   \text{Apples Xiao Hua has now} = 2 - 1 = 1
   \]

4. **Determine how many apples Xiao Ming has after receiving some from Xiao Hua:**
   - Xiao Ming originally had 3 apples.
   - He received 1 apple from Xiao Hua.
   \[
   \text{Apples Xiao Ming has now} = 3 + 1 = 4
   \]

Therefore, after Xiao Hua gives 1 apple to Xiao Ming, Xiao Ming has 4 apples.
