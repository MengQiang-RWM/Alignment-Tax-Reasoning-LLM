Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. **Initial number of apples:**
   - Xiao Ming starts with 3 apples.
   
2. **Number of apples Xiao Hong has:**
   - Xiao Hong has 2 more apples than Xiao Ming.
   - Therefore, Xiao Hong has \( 3 + 2 = 5 \) apples.

3. **Number of apples Xiao Hua has:**
   - Xiao Hua has half as many apples as Xiao Hong.
   - Therefore, Xiao Hua has \( \frac{5}{2} = 2.5 \) apples (since we can't have half an apple in this context).

4. **Apples given to Xiao Ming by Xiao Hua:**
   - Xiao Hua gives 1 apple to Xiao Ming.
   - So, Xiao Ming now has \( 3 - 1 = 2 \) apples.

Thus, after receiving 1 apple from Xiao Hua, Xiao Ming has 2 apples.
