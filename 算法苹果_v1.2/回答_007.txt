Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. **Initial number of apples:**
   - Xiao Ming has 3 apples.
   - Xiao Hong has 2 more apples than Xiao Ming.
     \[
     \text{Number of apples Xiao Hong has} = 3 + 2 = 5
     \]
   - Therefore, Xiao Hong has 5 apples.

2. **Apples given by Xiao Hua:**
   - Xiao Hua has half as many apples as Xiao Hong.
     \[
     \text{Number of apples Xiao Hua has} = \frac{5}{2} = 2.5
     \]

3. **Apples given away by Xiao Hua:**
   - Xiao Hua gives 1 apple to Xiao Ming.
     \[
     \text{Number of apples Xiao Ming receives} = 2.5 - 1 = 1.5
     \]

4. **Final number of apples Xiao Ming has:**
   - Xiao Ming initially had 3 apples.
   - After receiving 1 apple from Xiao Hua:
     \[
     \text{Total number of apples Xiao Ming has now} = 3 + 1 = 4
     \]

So, after giving 1 apple to Xiao Ming, Xiao Ming now has 4 apples.
