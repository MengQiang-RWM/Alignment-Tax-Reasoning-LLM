Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. **Initial number of apples:**
   - Xiao Ming has 3 apples.
   - Xiao Hong has 2 more apples than Xiao Ming.
     \[
     \text{Apples for Xiao Hong} = 3 + 2 = 5
     \]

2. **Number of apples Xiao Hua has:**
   - Xiao Hua has half as many apples as Xiao Hong.
     \[
     \text{Apples for Xiao Hua} = \frac{5}{2} = 2.5
     \]
   - Since we can't have half an apple in practical terms, let’s assume Xiao Hua has 2 apples (if fractional apples are not allowed).

3. **Total number of apples after giving one to Xiao Ming:**
   - Initially, Xiao Ming had 3 apples.
   - After giving 1 apple to Xiao Ming:
     \[
     \text{New number of apples for Xiao Ming} = 3 - 1 = 2
     \]

So, after giving 1 apple to Xiao Ming, Xiao Ming now has 2 apples.
