Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. **Initial number of apples:**
   - Xiao Ming has 3 apples.
   
2. **Number of apples Xiao Hong has:**
   - Xiao Hong has 2 more apples than Xiao Ming.
   - Therefore, Xiao Hong has \(3 + 2 = 5\) apples.

3. **Number of apples Xiao Hua has:**
   - Xiao Hua has half as many apples as Xiao Hong.
   - Therefore, Xiao Hua has \(\frac{5}{2} = 2.5\) apples (but since we can't have half an apple in this context, let's assume he has 2 or 3 apples).
   - So, Xiao Hua has either 2 or 3 apples.

4. **Apples given to Xiao Ming:**
   - Xiao Hua gives 1 apple to Xiao Ming.
   - Therefore, Xiao Ming now has \(3 - 1 = 2\) apples.

To summarize:
- Initially, Xiao Ming has 3 apples.
- Xiao Hong has 5 apples.
- If Xiao Hua gives 1 apple to Xiao Ming, then Xiao Ming will have 2 apples left.

So, after giving away 1 apple to Xiao Ming, Xiao Ming now has 2 apples.
