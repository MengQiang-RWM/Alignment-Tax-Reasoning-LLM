To determine how many apples Xiao Ming has after giving one apple to Xiao Ming, we will follow these steps:

   - Xiao Ming starts with 3 apples.
   - Xiao Hong has 2 more apples than Xiao Ming.

   - Xiao Hong has \(3 + 2 = 5\) apples.

3. **Calculate the number of apples Xiao Hua has:**
   - Xiao Hua has half as many apples as Xiao Hong.
   - Xiao Hua has \(\frac{5}{2} = 2.5\) apples (since he can't give half an apple).

4. **Determine the total number of apples Xiao Hua originally had before giving any away:**
   - Xiao Hua originally had 2.5 apples.

5. **Subtract the number of apples Xiao Hua gave to Xiao Ming:**
   - Xiao Ming gives 1 apple to Xiao Hua.
   - After giving 1 apple, Xiao Hua has \(2.5 - 1 = 1.5\) apples left.

6. **Add the number of apples Xiao Ming receives from Xiao Hua:**
   - Xiao Ming receives 1 apple from Xiao Hua.
   - Therefore, Xiao Ming now has \(3 + 1 = 4\) apples.

So, after giving 1 apple to Xiao Ming, Xiao Ming has \(\boxed{4}\) apples.
