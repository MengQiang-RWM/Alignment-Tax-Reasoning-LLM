   \[
   \text{Number of apples Xiao Hua has} = \frac{5}{2} = 2.5
   \]

   - Initially, Xiao Ming has 3 apples.
   - After giving one apple to Xiao Ming:
   \[
   \text{Apples left with Xiao Ming} = 3 - 1 = 2
