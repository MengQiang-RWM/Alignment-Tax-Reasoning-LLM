Let's break down the problem step by step:

1. **Initial number of apples:**
   - Xiao Ming starts with 3 apples.
   
2. **Apples for Xiao Hong:**
   - Xiao Hong has 2 more apples than Xiao Ming.
   - Therefore, Xiao Hong gets \(3 + 2 = 5\) apples.

3. **Apples for Xiao Hua:**
   - Xiao Hua has half as many apples as Xiao Hong.
   - Since Xiao Hong has 5 apples, Xiao Hua gets \(\frac{5}{2} = 2.5\) apples (but since we can't give half an apple, let’s assume it means giving 2 whole apples).

4. **Total apples before giving any to Xiao Ming:**
   - Adding up all the apples:
     - Xiao Ming: 3 apples
     - Xiao Hong: 5 apples
     - Xiao Hua: 2.5 apples

   Total: \(3 + 5 + 2.5 = 10.5\) apples

5. **Giving 1 apple to Xiao Ming:**
   - Xiao Ming receives 1 apple from Xiao Hua.
   - New total: \(3 + 5 + 2.5 + 1 = 11\) apples.

So, after giving 1 apple to Xiao Ming, Xiao Ming now has 11 apples.
