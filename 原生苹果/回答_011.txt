Let's start with the initial number of apples each person has:

- Xiao Ming has 3 apples.
- Xiao Hong has 2 more apples than Xiao Ming, so she has \(3 + 2 = 5\) apples.
- Xiao Hua has half as many apples as Xiao Hong, so he has \(\frac{5}{2} = 2.5\) apples.

Now, let's calculate how many apples each person has after Xiao Hua gives one apple to Xiao Ming:

- Xiao Ming receives 1 apple from Xiao Hua, so he now has \(3 - 1 = 2\) apples.
- Xiao Hong still has 5 apples since no apples were given away.
- Xiao Hua also receives 1 apple from Xiao Ming, so he now has \(2 + 1 = 3\) apples.

Therefore, after giving one apple to Xiao Ming, Xiao Ming has 2 apples left.
